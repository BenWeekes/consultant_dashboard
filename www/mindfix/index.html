What is MindFix?

A platform that helps mental health professionals support more people at a lower price point, without cutting corners.

Always Available

24/7 AI sessions with no scheduling needed. Get support whenever you need it, day or night.

Real-Time Wellbeing Monitoring

Voice and camera biomarkers are analysed during sessions to help understand your emotional state.

Therapist Oversight

Every client is registered with a licensed therapist who reviews session summaries, tracks your progress, guides the AI, and manages escalation.

How It Works

Three simple steps to start your wellness journey

1

Sign in securely

Log in with Google and verify your phone number. Your identity stays private and protected.

2

Talk to your AI companion

Start a private video session. Real-time biomarkers help gauge how you're feeling as you talk.

3

Review and follow-up

After each session, a summary is sent to your therapist. They review your progress, provide direction to the AI, and follow up when needed.
