What is MindFix?

A platform that helps therapists extend care between appointments, support more clients safely, and give each client access to help whenever they need it.

24/7 Client Access

Clients can start a guided AI session anytime, without waiting for the next appointment or needing to schedule in advance.

Real-Time Wellbeing Signals

Voice and camera biomarkers are analysed during sessions to help track stress, emotional state, and changes over time.

Therapist Oversight

Every client is linked to a licensed therapist who reviews session summaries, tracks progress, guides the AI, and stays involved when risk rises.

How It Works

A simple model for always-on client support with therapist visibility built in.

1

Sign in securely

Log in and start a session privately in the browser, with secure access and no app to install.

2

Talk to your AI companion

Talk through difficult moments in real time while MindFix tracks session context and optional wellbeing signals as you speak.

3

Therapist review and follow-up

After each session, therapists receive structured summaries, biomarker trends, and continuity context so they can guide care between appointments.

How Biomarkers Are Used

MindFix can use live voice and camera biomarkers to add context during a session, without letting the data take over the conversation.

What they are

MindFix can estimate signals such as stress, fatigue, HRV, breathing rate, heart rate, and emotion trends from voice and camera input during a session.

How they help

These signals give the AI and therapist extra context, helping them notice patterns that may be worth exploring when something stands out.

What they are not

Biomarkers are supportive cues, not diagnoses. MindFix is designed to be therapist-first and biomarker-aware second, using the data only when it adds value.

For Mental Health Professionals

MindFix does not replace the therapist relationship. It helps you support more clients, reduce blind time between sessions, and stay in control of continuity, summaries, and escalation.
